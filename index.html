Our Technology

Proprietary Products & Platforms

Gossamer has pioneered several patented structural and optical technologies that deliver unmatched performance in concentrated solar power, architectural systems, and scientific platforms. Each product is engineered for minimal LCOE, maximum precision, and rapid deployment.

Flagship Product

LAT™ — Large Aperture Trough Series

The most advanced commercial CSP trough system available globally. The LAT series delivers industry-leading aperture widths and optical efficiency, dramatically lowering levelized cost of energy (LCOE) for utility-scale solar thermal plants.

LAT1 Aperture
7.3 m
Receiver Tube

  • 20% improvement in operating efficiency vs. prior generation
  • LAT2 in development: 10m aperture / 90mm receiver, targeting PV parity
  • 3rd & 4th generation designs in active R&D
  • No on-site assembly fixtures, machinery, or buildings required

Organic Connector™

Patented joint system providing unparalleled frame accuracy and rigidity. Eliminates initial and ongoing alignment requirements.

Sunlock™ Tracker

The most powerful and precise solar drive system available. Erected as a complete unit with exceptional torsional stiffness.

X-PERF™ Reflectors

World's largest single-facet parabolic mirrors. Installs in 5 man-minutes. Glass-preserving support structure eliminates breakage risk.

About Gossamer

Gossamer Space Frames is a California-based engineering and structural systems company with over 30 years of experience delivering precision solutions for the world's most demanding solar, architectural, and scientific projects.

Our mission is to explore the world of technical possibilities — creating the best answers to meet the fundamental needs of our clients and the world around us. We've built an "innovation engine" that continuously derives and conveys new solutions to present-day applications.
